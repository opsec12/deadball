# Dead Ball

A one-hour murder mystery game.

Friday, September 18, 2026. During the Abilene vs. Chapman football game, a man is killed in an office behind the bleachers. Eight people are on the suspect list. The killer is already gone, and the trail crosses two continents and sixteen locations.

## How to play

1. Download `index.html`.
2. Double-click it to open it in your browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari).
3. Type the passphrase to unlock the game.
4. Click **Open the case**.

The passphrase is not in this repository. Ask the owner for it.

## Tips

- Every answer is hidden in the evidence.
- Research is allowed. Look up anything you don't know.
- Hints cost points, so use them wisely.
- Tap any picture to enlarge it.
- Use the Case File, Notebook and Cipher Desk in the top bar.
- Clear the innocent suspects before you accuse anyone.

## Notes

- It works offline, except for the translator links.
- It needs an up-to-date browser.
- The game plays music. Use the Music button to turn it off.
- Progress is not saved. Every time you open the file, you start at the beginning.

This is a work of fiction.
